02

C'est une donnée du secteur, pas une mesure de notre part.Academic research on AI in small and mid-sized companies (UOC, 2025) puts the drop in information errors on routine queries at 40% to 50%, from lower fatigue and more consistency. That is an industry finding, not a measurement of ours.
